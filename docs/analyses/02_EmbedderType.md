### Comparative Table of Embedding Model Types

| Type / Embedder Family | Mechanism & Primary Usage | Specific Models (Names) | Concrete Use Case / Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dense Bi-Encoders (SBERT)** | Converts an entire passage into a single **dense vector** (e.g., 384 to 1024 dimensions). Ideal for massive indexing and fast vector search. | `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`, `bge-large-en-v1.5`, `multilingual-e5-large`, `paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2` | **RAG System (Stage 1)**: Index millions of documents in a vector database (Chroma, Qdrant) and retrieve the top 50 relevant documents in milliseconds. |
| **Cross-Encoders (Re-rankers)** | Generates **no vectors in advance**. Directly evaluates the relevance score of a `(query, passage)` pair. Highly accurate but computationally slower. | `bge-reranker-v2-m3`, `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`, `cohere-rerank-v3` (API) | **Re-ranking**: Take the top 20 results retrieved by a Bi-Encoder and re-score them to select only the top 3 to 5 most precise contexts before passing them to the LLM. |
| **Hybrid / Multi-Function Models** | Modern models capable of generating dense embeddings, sparse keyword weights, and handling long contexts (up to 8k tokens) simultaneously. | `bge-m3`, `gte-Qwen2`, `mxbai-embed-large` | **Unified Search Engine**: Perform multilingual retrieval across long PDF reports without requiring aggressive text chunking. |
| **Sparse Encoders (SPLADE)** | Generates **sparse vectors** mapped to a vocabulary. Assigns importance weights to keywords while expanding them with semantic synonyms. | `naver/splade-v3`, `prithivida/Splade_PP_en_v1` | **Technical / Exact-Match Search**: Retrieve specific product codes (e.g., `REF-980X`), medical or legal terminology, and part numbers that dense embeddings might blur out. |
| **Multi-Vector Models (ColBERT)** | Retains **one vector per token** instead of compressing the whole sentence into a single vector. Compares queries and documents via late interaction. | `ColBERTv2`, `ColPali` (optimized for visual/PDF documents) | **Fine-Grained Document Retrieval**: Pinpoint highly specific information hidden in footnotes, complex tables, or diagrams without losing context. |
| **Proprietary Embedders (APIs)** | Closed-source models hosted behind paid APIs. Excellent overall performance without requiring GPU infrastructure management. | `text-embedding-3-small / large` (OpenAI), `embed-multilingual-v3.0` (Cohere), `voyage-3` (Voyage AI) | **Prototyping & Cloud Applications**: Quickly deploy production-ready RAG applications without hosting or maintaining open-source models. |
